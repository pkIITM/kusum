About Us

Plateful & Platter is a newly launched small-scale cloud kitchen based in Supaul, Bihar. We specialize in home-style meals and tasty quick bites. You can now order from us on Swiggy and Zomato. Our aim is to serve quality food made with care and hygiene. Whether it's your breakfast, lunch, or evening snacks — we've got you covered!
